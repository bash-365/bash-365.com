Correction to our QuickBooks-to-ERP piece: QuickBooks Online companies don't consolidate on their own, but Intuit documents a route in QuickBooks Online Advanced, multi-company reporting through Spreadsheet Sync in Excel. It still means manual labor every reporting cycle.

We've updated the article to say that precisely, and to state the implementation timeline as a range of roughly half a year rather than a fixed span. The test for finance is unchanged: put a dollar figure on reconciliation labor, a delayed close and error risk, then compare it to the cost of moving.

Read the updated piece before your next close.
